PROPOSAL

A redesigned web and shopping experience featuring social selling for back-end, affluent millennials in order for them to connect with Birk’s brand and product values. The end goal is to increase perceived value of Birks’ brand/products and establish a more sustainable market for the future, such that Birks is not left behind during the jewelry industry’s massive market shift.

My role was industry research, UI/Web design, visual design and communication.
