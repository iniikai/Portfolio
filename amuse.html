INTRODUCTION

Amuse is a conceptual camera provided by iStock to contracted clients for them to take pictures and videos of their everyday life to provide as sellable stock material on their online marketplace. For every stock photo or footage that is sold on the marketplace, the contracted clients get a cut of the profits deposited directly into their account.

A real-time on-screen display reflects the estimated selling price of what the lens is pointing at based on current market demands: factors that may affect the price include composition, number of people in the photo, the demographic and gender of people in the photo, etc.

REMOTES

This product comes with two remotes, a small foot remote and a hand remote, to take natural images anytime from anywhere. Inside each remote there is a bluetooth device.

CRITIQUE

With our project we aim to critique change of behavior and trading privacy for money.
